SEC 01 — The Hull

Buoyancy, engineered.

A double hull of bio-concrete and recycled composite rides the swell like a leaf. Ballast lagoons at the core shift weight in real time, keeping every street level within half a degree — even in open water.

Draft — 14.2 m

02

SEC 02 — The Skin

A surface that works.

Every exposed face harvests. Photovoltaic petals track the sun, wind columns breathe through the towers, and a titanium-dioxide skin scrubs CO₂ from the air the way a reef filters water.

Output — 34 MW

SEC 03 — The Gardens

Food inside the hull.

Terraced aquaculture lagoons and vertical farms grow most of the district's calories on board. The central lagoon collects rain, buffers heat, and serves as the city's ballast heart.

Yield — 80 % on-hull

Arrival, choreographed.

Docking rings on the leeward edge receive zero-emission shuttles and cargo gliders. The district drifts with seasonal currents — a city that travels, yet your address never changes.

Sheet 03 — Inquiry log

How a district floats,
and how it holds.

Q.01How does a city float?

Displacement — the same physics as a ship, at district scale. The double hull encloses more air than the city weighs, and ballast lagoons at the core pump water between chambers to trim the deck level. Stability is managed the way an offshore platform manages it: continuously, automatically, redundantly.

Q.02What happens in a storm?

The district is engineered to Category-5 offshore standards. When the forecast passes threshold, edge decks seal, the harbor ring closes, and the ballast system lowers the profile against the wind. Residents shelter in place — the safest place in a hurricane at sea is inside a hull built for one.

Q.03Who is Lilypad for?

Coastal communities facing managed retreat, residents displaced by rising water, and the researchers and crews who run the district's systems. The housing charter is mixed-income by design: a floating city that only the wealthy can board is a yacht, not a city.

Q.04When can I move in?

The pilot district — one central pad, two satellite pads, 1,200 residents — is targeted for 2031. The first full ring follows in 2035. Berth reservations for the pilot open with the program's next funding phase.
